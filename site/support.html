Flow Now

One person builds these apps. If something is broken, wrong, or missing, email me and a real person will read it and write back.

Get in touch

Email info at glance-works dot com. Include your device and iOS version if it's a bug, and the river or gauge if it's about a specific reading — it makes it much faster to track down.

Common things

  • A gauge shows no data. The USGS feed for that site is down or reporting gaps; it usually returns on its own. If it stays blank for more than a day, let me know which gauge.
  • The widget isn't updating. iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Opening the app forces a fresh reading.
  • You can't find your river. Search by river name, town, or zip code. If a gauge you know exists isn't showing up, email me the USGS site number and I'll look into it.
